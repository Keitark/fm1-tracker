This dedication applies only to these generated original assets. It does not
change the license of the repository's software, external SDK, imported MODs,
or any third-party work.

All waveforms are deterministic mathematical synthesis. The break chops use
newly synthesized drums and original hit timings. COME ON!, HEY! and YEAH! are
original robotic formant synthesis, not recordings or pretrained speech output.
No famous break loop, classic MOD sample or recognizable sampled vocal is used.
